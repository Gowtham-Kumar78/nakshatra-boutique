About the boutique

Nakshatra Star Boutique is a local boutique in Duvvada, Visakhapatnam. We design, stitch and style outfits for the occasions that matter to you.

The name comes from the nakshatras, the stars of the Indian sky. Every customer gets a piece that feels like theirs.

Come in with an occasion. Leave with an outfit that fits you.

What we make

  • Custom tailoringMade-to-measure stitching and alterations.
  • Sarees and blousesSilk, cotton and designer sarees, with blouses stitched to fit.
  • Ethnic wearLehengas, salwar sets, kurtis and festive outfits.
  • Bridal and festiveOutfits planned around your wedding or festival date.
